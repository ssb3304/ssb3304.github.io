02. Research

My research lies at the intersection of neuroscience and engineering, focusing on understanding and interfacing with the human brain.

BCI / BMI

Brain-Computer & Brain-Machine Interfaces enabling direct neural communication with external devices.

Neural Encoding & Decoding

Decoding vision and motor control signals from neural activity to understand brain representations.

Cognitive Neuroscience

Investigating the neural mechanisms underlying cognition, perception, and decision-making.

Brain Imaging

Leveraging neuroimaging modalities (EEG, fMRI) to observe and analyze brain structure and function.
